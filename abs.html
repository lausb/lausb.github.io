In Problem 5 of the Exam, we need an additional condition that \(f\) is differentiable. The correct version of the problem is:

  • Let \(f : \mathbb {R}^m \rightarrow \mathbb {R}\) be differentiable. If \(f\) has bounded gradient \(\nabla f\), that is \[ \beta = \sup _{x \in \mathbb {R}^m} | \nabla f (x) | < \infty \text {,} \] show that \(f : \mathbb {R}^m \rightarrow \mathbb {R}\) is uniformly continuous.
